مقال كل يوم يفرق معاك

ReadIn منصة معرفة بالعربي والإنجليزي. الكُتّاب ينشرون فيها مقالات منسقة بعناية، والقرّاء يبنون عادة قراءة مقال مفيد واحد كل يوم في المجال الذي يحبونه. يعرض التطبيق النص العربي المختلط بالإنجليزي بشكل صحيح، فتبقى المصطلحات وعلامات الترقيم والأكواد في مكانها. نبدأ بمجال البرمجيات: الذكاء الاصطناعي، وتعلّم الآلة، و.NET، والواجهات الأمامية، وتطبيقات الموبايل، وقواعد البيانات، وDevOps، ومعمارية البرمجيات، والأمان، والاختبارات.

ReadIn

Read daily. Grow daily.

ReadIn is a bilingual Arabic and English knowledge app. Writers publish well-formatted articles, and readers build a habit of reading one valuable article a day in the field they love. It gets mixed Arabic and English text right, so technical terms, punctuation and code stay in the right order. ReadIn starts with software: AI, machine learning, .NET, frontend, mobile, databases, DevOps, software architecture, security and testing.

What's inside

  • ☕

    Today's article

    One article picked for you every day from your interests, with a reminder at the time you choose.

  • 🔥

    Streaks and reading stats

    Your reading streak, a heatmap of your reading days, minutes read and your top fields.

  • 🧭

    Learning paths

    Ordered series of articles on one topic, with your progress and the next article.

  • ↔️

    Arabic and English, done right

    Each paragraph keeps its own direction, English terms sit cleanly inside Arabic text, and code is always left to right.

  • 🌐

    Two versions of each article

    Egyptian Arabic with English terms, or English only. Pick the one that suits you.

  • ✓

    Trust you can see

    Verified writers, reviewed articles, sources on every technical article, and a clear label on AI-assisted articles.

  • ✍️

    Write and discuss

    A rich editor, comments and reactions, following writers, and saving articles for later.

  • 🌙

    Light and dark

    Light mode, dark mode, or follow your device setting.
